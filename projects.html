Bulk RNA sequencing pipeline for wood frog skin transcriptomics

MSc thesis, Katzenback Lab, University of Waterloo · 2021 – 2024 · github.com/dthom7/MScThesisCode

For my MSc, I built the analysis pipeline used to study how the dorsal skin of the North American wood frog (Rana sylvatica) changes through development and in response to chronic exposure to a microplastics mixture. The pipeline runs on the Slurm-managed clusters of the Digital Research Alliance of Canada and covers quality control, alignment, transcriptome assembly, functional annotation, differential expression analysis, and gene set enrichment analysis.

  • Processed over 3 billion raw reads from 34 samples, and assembled 96,645 transcripts across 45,926 genes.
  • Adapted the pipeline from a planned de novo assembly to a reference-guided approach when the wood frog reference genome was released partway through the analysis.
  • Wrote the pipeline as approximately 50 Slurm-driven Bash scripts and 34 R analysis scripts, using strict-mode Bash, array jobs, md5 integrity checks at every stage, and pinned tool versions so each run can be reproduced.

The findings are being prepared for publication, so I'll add a summary here once the papers are out.

Environmental DNA metabarcoding and amphibian skin microbiome analysis

Katzenback Lab, University of Waterloo · 2022 – 2025

As a Research Technician, I performed full stack analysis of custom environmental DNA metabarcoding data, from raw sequencing reads through to results, for collaborative projects surveying amphibian communities. During my MSc, I also analyzed 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing data from larval wood frog skin swabs to characterize the skin microbiome, including testing a blocking primer to reduce host amplification.

This work is ongoing or in preparation for publication, so details will follow.

Clustal Omega for Apple Silicon

Personal project · github.com/dthom7/clustal-omega-apple-silicon

I ported the Clustal Omega multiple sequence alignment tool to Apple Silicon. Along the way, I tracked down and fixed a concurrency bug in the OpenMP ktuple pairwise-distance routine that appeared when the thread count was a multiple of 4.

Cancer genome analysis pipelines in WDL and Cromwell

Shlien Lab, The Hospital for Sick Children · 2018 – 2019

During my co-op term, I researched ways to make the Shlien Research Group's cancer genome analysis pipelines easier to maintain, port, and use, and rewrote them in WDL and Cromwell with documentation for running and extending them. I returned as a Technologist to maintain the pipelines, add new features, and work with a third-party company on running the analyses in the cloud. Somatic variant calling for a 70-genome leiomyosarcoma cohort was run through these pipelines, contributing to Anderson et al. (2021) in Nature Communications.

ActiveDriverWGS (original version)

Reimand Lab, Ontario Institute for Cancer Research · 2017

I co-developed the original ActiveDriverWGS R package for discovering cancer driver mutations in non-coding DNA from whole genomes, packaging the Reimand Research Group's R scripts into a conventional R package. The software is credited in Zhu et al. (2020) in Molecular Cell. The lab has since re-implemented the package.
