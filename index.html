Drew Thompson

Bioinformatician. MSc Biology and BCS Bioinformatics, University of Waterloo.

I work at the intersection of biology and computing. My training spans bioinformatics, molecular biology, immunology, and ecotoxicology, and most of my work has been building and running analysis pipelines for sequencing data, including bulk RNA sequencing, 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing, environmental DNA metabarcoding, and cancer whole genomes.

I completed my MSc in the Katzenback Lab at the University of Waterloo as part of the pELAstics project at the IISD Experimental Lakes Area, a multi-institution collaboration studying the fate and effects of microplastics in freshwater lakes. My thesis used transcriptomics and microbiome analysis to evaluate how a microplastics mixture affects the skin of developing wood frogs, and it received the Dean of Science Award in Biology. Before that, my bioinformatics co-op terms took me through cancer genomics at The Hospital for Sick Children and the Ontario Institute for Cancer Research, plant genome annotation at Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada, and iOS development.

I'm looking for bioinformatics and computational biology roles, particularly in pharmaceutical and biotech research.
